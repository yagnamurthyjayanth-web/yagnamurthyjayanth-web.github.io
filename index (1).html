How I take a product from requirement to release.

It always starts with the client, not with a solution.

01

Requirements

Gather needs from clients in interviews and workshops.

02

Research

Benchmark vendors and competitors, map journeys and flows.

03

Plan

Roadmap, prioritised backlog, and a project plan from the SOW.

04

Design

Wireframes, UX/UI and prototypes with the design team, reviewed and signed off by the client.

05

Build

Scrum sprints with planning, stand-ups, refinement and reviews.

06

Validate

UAT with business users and CAB review before go-live.

07

Release & report

Sign-off, release, then weekly status and monthly reviews.

What I do in each role.

Requirements, product and project work, side by side.

Requirements & research

05
  • Gather requirements from clients through interviews and workshops with functional, L&D and leadership teams
  • Turn client inputs and scope documents into prioritised, build-ready requirements
  • Benchmark competitor and vendor platforms to set scope and integration paths
  • Map user journeys, information architecture and process flows in Figma and FigJam
  • Research-led page design, presented to and approved by client leadership

Product management

08
  • Own product vision, roadmap and priorities once requirements are agreed
  • Manage and prioritise the backlog by business value, effort and dependencies
  • Write epics, user stories, acceptance criteria and BRDs
  • Run Scrum ceremonies: planning, stand-ups, refinement, reviews, retros
  • Lead UX/UI design reviews: wireframes, prototypes and client walkthroughs before build
  • Define MVP and release scope; coordinate UAT and sign-off
  • Lead build-vs-buy decisions with clear trade-offs
  • Track product KPIs and report to leadership

Project management

05
  • Translate each signed SOW into a detailed project plan: scope, milestones, resourcing, timelines
  • Track delivery against plan in monday.com
  • Weekly status reports with risks, owners and ETAs; monthly business reviews
  • Run the weekly Change Advisory Board with internal and vendor teams
  • Vendor outreach and partnerships through corporate approval chains
